**Team:** Azaan Asif Chowdhary and Ayaan Choudhary
**Judging criteria covered:** ease of registration, language accessibility, inclusive design, queue-management efficiency, navigation accuracy, emergency handling, technical execution, real-world usefulness.

---

## Before the timer starts (do this in 60 seconds)

### 1. Open three windows in the **same browser profile**

| Window | URL | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left | `YOUR-SITE/#/opd` | Patient phone |
| Middle | `YOUR-SITE/#/staff` | Staff dashboard |
| Right | `YOUR-SITE/#/board` | Waiting-room TV board |

Do **not** use two browsers or two devices: the shared queue only syncs between tabs of the same browser.

### 2. Reset the demo

On the staff dashboard, press **Reset everything** (सब कुछ रीसेट करें) and confirm. This returns all three windows to a clean demo queue and resets the language choice.

### 3. Pre-flight checks

- [ ] Patient window shows the **Choose your language** screen.
- [ ] Zoom is **100%**; the browser toolbar and bookmarks bar are hidden.
- [ ] Staff window shows the 7 department cards and **Urgent flags** area.
- [ ] Board window is in **full-screen** (`Open full-screen board`).
- [ ] Press the **Listen** button once to unlock audio.
- [ ] In the patient window, open **Settings** once and grant **Phone alerts** if you plan to show a notification. Then close Settings.
- [ ] On a phone, enable vibration and media volume. On a laptop, vibration is not available — the in-app SMS banner is the fallback.
- [ ] Screen recording is running (backup proof if the network drops).

## The 3-minute script

### 0:00–0:15 — The problem (15 sec)

> **Say:** “At a government hospital, Kamla spends her morning in one queue to register, then gets a paper slip she cannot read — and cannot find the room on it. OPD Saathi gives her the queue and the way in her own language, on a basic phone.”
>
> **Problem it solves:** registration and wayfinding are two of the biggest, most avoidable costs of an OPD visit.

**Do:** Gesture to the three windows. Do not click yet.

---

### 0:15–0:25 — Three screens, one queue (10 sec)

> **Say:** “Three windows, one live queue: the patient on the left, the staff dashboard in the middle, and the waiting-room board on the right. What staff call appears on the patient's phone and on the hall TV at the same time.”
>
> **Problem it solves:** today patients crowd the corridor because only the staff know the queue.

**Do:** Point to each window, in order.

---

### 0:25–1:05 — Register in Hindi, in assisted mode (40 sec)
*Criteria: ease of registration, language accessibility, inclusive design*

> **Say:** “Language is the first decision, before any form — because a patient cannot consent to a form they can't read.”

**Do:**
1. On the patient window, language gate → tap **हिंदी**.
2. Tap **रजिस्टर करें** (Register).
3. Toggle **कोई मेरी मदद कर रहा है** (Someone is helping me) ON.  
   **Say:** “Assisted mode enlarges the text, turns on read-aloud and turns on stair-free routes, because Kamla may not be the one filling the form.”
4. Tap **कमला की जानकारी भरें · 62** to fill the demo details (or type **कमला**, age **62**, choose **महिला**).
5. Tap **आगे** (Next) through age, gender and mobile.
6. On the last step, tap **जनरल मेडिसिन** — the card says *बुखार, खाँसी, बीपी, शुगर* (fever, cough, BP, sugar).
7. Tap **मेरी ओपीडी पर्ची बनाएँ** (Get my OPD slip).

> **Say:** “Five one-question steps, picture cards in plain Hindi, a helper mode and speech input. The registration queue becomes five taps.”
>
> **Problem it solves:** elderly and low-literacy patients no longer need a staff member to decode a long English form.

---

### 1:05–1:20 — The OPD slip (15 sec)
*Criteria: technical execution, real-world usefulness*

> **Say:** “The paper slip becomes one screen: token, first name, department, room and floor, doctor, people ahead, estimated wait, and a QR code that opens her place in line. She can print it, save it, or show it in large type.”

**Do:** Point to the token (for example **MED-046**), the QR code and **मेरी बारी खोलें** (Open my turn). Tap it.

> **Problem it solves:** there is no ambiguity about where to go or what to show.

---

### 1:20–1:50 — “3 ahead”, the board and staff actions (30 sec)
*Criteria: queue-management efficiency, technical execution*

**Do:**
1. In the staff window, find **General Medicine** and press **Call next** (अगले को बुलाएँ) **two to three times**, until the patient screen shows the close-to-turn banner.
2. On the patient window, point to:
   - **आपकी बारी पास है** (Your turn is close)
   - the **simulated SMS** panel
   - the live position and estimated wait
3. Point to the board: the called token is now-serving, the next three are listed, and the board switches between Hindi and English every six seconds.
4. **Optional if time:** press **Delay +10** on the staff General Medicine card. The patient's estimate updates.  
   **Say:** “One staff click reports a delay once; every screen and the board update — no shouting names.”

> **Say:** “When three or fewer people are ahead, the phone alerts her: vibrate, sound, banner and a simulated SMS. The board carries the same call for anyone without a phone.”
>
> **Problem it solves:** patients stop crowding the door, and staff stop repeating the same announcement all morning.

---

### 1:50–2:20 — Avoid-stairs route with landmarks (30 sec)
*Criteria: navigation accuracy, inclusive design*

> **Say:** “Now the part after the slip — actually finding Room 1.”

**Do:**
1. On the patient window, go to **जगह खोजें** (Find a place).  
   (If the header is in the way, use the back/home control and choose **जगह खोजें**.)
2. Set **Where are you now?** to **मुख्य प्रवेश द्वार** (Main entrance).
3. Set **Where do you want to go?** to **ओपीडी कमरा 1** (OPD room 1).
4. Make sure **सीढ़ियाँ नहीं** (Avoid stairs) is **ON**.
5. Tap **मुझे रास्ता दिखाएँ** (Show me the way).
6. Point at the inline SVG floor plan: the blue route goes to the lift, not the stairs.
7. Scroll to **एक-एक कदम** (Step by step). Read one landmark out loud:  
   *“आप ओपीडी कमरा 1 पहुँच गए हैं। यह नीली कुर्सियों के सामने है।”*  
   (You have reached OPD Room 1. It is opposite the blue chairs.)
8. Tap **सुनें** (Listen) to hear the steps.
9. Tap **मैं भूल गया/गई हूँ** (I'm lost). Show the nearest-help-desk route and the large card that shows the destination in Hindi and English.

> **Say:** “The route comes from Dijkstra's shortest path over the hospital's three-floor walking graph. Avoid stairs removes every stair edge and uses lifts and ramps. Every step carries a landmark she can actually see — and if the map fails, the bilingual card gets a human to help.”
>
> **Problem it solves:** ‘Room 3’ on a slip stops being a dead end.

---

### 2:20–2:40 — Urgent flag, then Emergency (20 sec)
*Criteria: emergency handling*

> **Say:** “One thing the app deliberately does not do is decide who is an emergency. Only hospital staff do that.”

**Do:**
1. On the patient window, tap the floating **मुझे तुरंत मदद चाहिए** (I need urgent help) button.
2. Choose **मेरी तबीयत अभी बहुत खराब है** (I feel very unwell now) and tap **झंडा भेजें** (Send urgent flag).
3. Switch attention to the staff window: the flag is pinned at the top, with a new-alert sound and unseen count. Press **देख लिया** (Mark as seen).
4. Open the red **इमरजेंसी** button on the patient window. Show:
   - the eight danger signs
   - **112** and **108**
   - the Casualty route
   - **“इमरजेंसी के लिए टोकन की ज़रूरत नहीं है”** (You do not need a token for Emergency)

> **Say:** “A patient raises one flag and staff see it until they acknowledge it. A real emergency goes straight to Casualty with the number and the route — no token, no registration, and no software second-guessing a clinician.”
>
> **Problem it solves:** an urgent case cannot get lost behind a routine queue or a form.

---

### 2:40–3:00 — Close (20 sec)
*Criteria: technical execution, real-world usefulness*

> **Say:** “It's one browser-based prototype with no backend: a real three-floor hospital graph and shortest-path routing, a five-step bilingual registration, a live queue shared across tabs, a staff command centre and a hall board. It gives patients back their time and their way — and it gives staff a calmer queue. It never replaces a clinician's judgement. Thank you.”
>
> **Problem it solves:** it makes a government OPD visit understandable, accessible and predictable — for the people who need that most.

**Do:** Step back and leave all three windows visible for questions.

## Judge-facing summary

| Criterion | What to show | Why it stands out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ease of registration | 5 one-question steps, draft saved, helper mode | Shorter and less intimidating than a paper counter form |
| Language accessibility | Hindi from the first screen, including landmarks and board announcements | Language is a first decision, not a settings afterthought |
| Inclusive design | Assisted mode, large targets, high contrast, read aloud, avoid stairs, I'm lost | Designed for Kamla, not for a typical app user |
| Queue efficiency | Now-serving, next 3, delay, no-show, transfer, overcrowding | Gives staff one control surface and patients one truth |
| Navigation accuracy | Dijkstra over a real 3-floor graph; route drawn on inline SVG; landmarks | Routes are computed, not decorative; stairs are genuinely excluded |
| Emergency handling | Emergency on every screen, 8 danger signs, 112/108, urgent flag with acknowledgement | Never blocks emergency care behind a token; never triages by software |
| Technical execution | React/Vite, localStorage + BroadcastChannel, bilingual i18n, no backend | Works live across tabs, accessible, deployable as a static site |
| Real-world usefulness | The full visit: register → slip → wait → navigate → urgent → staff | Solves a real end-to-end problem, not a single screen |

## Live-demo risks and how to avoid them

| Risk | Why it happens | How to avoid it | If it still happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Different data in each window** | Windows are in different browsers or profiles, or one is incognito | Use the **same browser profile**, three tabs/windows | Move the patient tab into the same browser as the staff tab and reset |
| **Old data from a previous run** | `localStorage` keeps the last queue | Press **Reset everything** on the staff dashboard before the timer | Reset live; it takes one click |
| **Patient tab shows the language gate mid-demo** | Reset clears the language choice | Choose Hindi at the start; do not reset after choosing | Tap **हिंदी** and continue |
| **“3 ahead” alert doesn’t appear** | The near-turn logic runs on the **My turn** screen, which must be open | Keep **मेरी बारी** open before calling next | Reopen My turn, then press **Call next** again |
| **No sound or vibration** | Autoplay, desktop hardware, or permissions | Click once in the page first; enable **Phone alerts**; use a phone for vibration | Point to the **bilingual SMS banner**, which is in-app and always visible |
| **No browser notification** | Permission was never granted, or notifications are blocked | Grant permission in pre-flight; test a flag before the demo | Narrate the banner: “On a phone this is also a notification” |
| **Microphone not available** | Speech recognition is not supported in that browser or is blocked | Use Chrome desktop; if the mic button is absent, type instead | Use the **Kamla demo chip** to fill without speaking |
| **QR camera scan not available** | `BarcodeDetector` is missing or camera permission denied | Do not rely on scanning; type the token | Use the **I have my slip** field and type the token |
| **No audio for Listen / board announcement** | Speech synthesis needs a user gesture or has no Hindi voice | Click **Listen** once in pre-flight; check volume and output device | Read the on-screen text aloud yourself |
| **Hindi does not fit or looks broken** | Browser font fallback or zoomed page | Use a current Chrome/Edge; keep 100% zoom | Switch to English for the demo and mention Hindi is fully implemented |
| **Tokens or rooms don’t match what you rehearsed** | Reset changes sample data and new tokens get the next number | Read the live token from the screen, not from notes | Say “for example, MED-046” and point at the screen |
| **Someone else opens the public staff link and changes the queue** | No real login in the demo | Keep the staff URL private; reset just before the demo; enable deployment password protection | Reset and continue |
| **Build works locally but not on the host** | Node version too old for Vite 7 | Set Node **22.x / 24.x** on Vercel, **22** on Netlify | Use the backup local build (`npm run dev`) |
| **Refreshing a route shows 404** | Missing SPA rewrite | `vercel.json` / `netlify.toml` in this repo already fix it | Deploy the included config; keep the judges on the `/#/` links |
| **Network drops during the demo** | Venue Wi-Fi | Have the backup recording and a local `npm run dev` ready; hotspot on a charged phone | Switch to the recording, then answer questions |
| **Laptop sleeps or notifications interrupt** | Long judging session | Plug in power, disable sleep, close chat apps, use Do Not Disturb | Wake and reset the demo |
| **Too many tabs / accidental clicks** | Six windows open | Keep only patient, staff and board; use one browser | Close the rest and reset |
| **Emergency `tel:` link opens a dialler** | 112/108 are real call links | Do **not** click them during the demo; point to them | Close the dialler and continue |
| **Board speaks over the presenter** | Speech synthesis announces called tokens | Keep one board tab; do not call tokens while talking about the board | Mute the system audio or close the board after showing it |
| **Accidental double registration** | Curiosity or a repeated click | Register once; the demo chip stays available | Reset and re-register; it takes under a minute |

## 30-second recovery script if something breaks

> “This is a live, client-side prototype, so let me reset the demo and take you straight to the patient flow.”  
> — press **Reset everything**, reopen `/#/opd`, choose **हिंदी**, and continue from registration.  
> If the network is down, switch to the recorded demonstration and keep answering questions from the app.
